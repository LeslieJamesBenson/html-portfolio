LJ's Favorite Movies of all Time

Blades of Glory

The comedy in this film is top-tier Will Ferrell & Jon Heder. The story is an outlier to traditional narratives and the jokes are ahead of their time. A classic that will be remembered for generations to come.

The Green Mile

The Green Mile is a powerful and emotional film that explores themes of justice, hope, and the human condition. It's a masterpiece of storytelling and acting and one of best of the great Tom Hanks.

The Italian Job

The Italian Job is a thrilling heist film that combines humor, action, and clever plotting. It's a standout in the genre and showcases the talents of its ensemble cast. Plus, Marky Mark is my favorite action star. It helps that I was his caddy when he came to the Coeur d'Alene golf resort.
