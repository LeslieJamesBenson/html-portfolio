About Me

My experience has taught me how to build trust quickly, understand what people actually need, and turn conversations into meaningful outcomes for both the client and the business. I bring a mix of professionalism, emotional intelligence, adaptability, and natural communication skills to every project, whether that means handling outreach, qualifying leads, setting appointments, writing persuasive messaging, or helping businesses improve the way they connect with people.

A large part of my background has been built in high-level sales environments, where I’ve developed strong skills in rapport-building, objection handling, strategic questioning, and guiding conversations with confidence and clarity. I’m especially strong in roles that require a human touch—work where sounding natural, understanding nuance, and creating real trust matters just as much as performance. Through freelancing, I’ve continued to build on those strengths by helping with client-facing communication, outreach, messaging, and sales support in ways that are organized, reliable, and results-focused.

I also love my dogs, Cleo and Athena, who are a huge part of my life and a reminder of what matters most to me: loyalty, connection, and creating a lifestyle that feels both meaningful and real.
